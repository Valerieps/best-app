# ADR-046: Day note focus lives in the settings

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Decision:** Focus mode on a part of the day note is stored in the settings record, as `dayNoteFocus: { day, part, writable }`, through `setDayNoteFocus`. `part` is the key the day note already uses to order its parts (a section id, or the priorities and "De outros dias" keys of `domain/order.ts`). Focusing the day's first section before it is saved stores its placeholder key, and the screen swaps it for the section id once the section exists. While the setting is present and the route is the day note, `App` shows that day's note in focus whatever the date, skipping "Iniciar Dia", warm up and dashboard, and hides the backup band; the day note shows only the focused part, without its header or notices. `writable` records whether the focus started on today's note, and keeps new lines allowed in that part after midnight (ADR-018 limits new lines to today's note). Nothing clears the setting except the user's toggle; a part that no longer exists shows a short message with the button to leave.
**Why:** The owner asked that focus survive reloading and that the app never leave it on its own, so a new day does not interrupt a train of thought (spec/nota-do-dia.md, "Modo foco"). The settings record already keeps interface choices that must persist, like the collapsed sidebar, and travels with the backup; `localStorage` would be lost with the browser data and is not part of the backup. Keeping the decision in `App` is what lets a focus outlive midnight, since the opening screens are chosen there.
