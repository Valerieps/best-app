# ADR-039: Reviews without a calendar: open record, cadence and signals

**Status:** Superseded by ADR-060
**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Decision:** The Revisão de Foco and the Revisão de Rumo are stored in `reflections` with `kind: 'focus' | 'direction'`. While a review is in progress its record has a fixed id (`focus:open`, `direction:open`), so the texts can be saved without listing the table inside a transaction; `closeReview` puts a closed copy under `kind:<new id>`, with `start` (the day the previous one closed) and `end` (today), deletes the open record and records `reflection.closed` with `{ kind, since, metrics }`. A closed review never reopens. The old `week` and `month` records stay as history and count as the last Revisão de Foco and Revisão de Rumo (`reviewKindOf`). Each review's period runs from the `closedAt` of the last closed review of its kind (or from the first event) until now, compared by event time (`at`), not by `day`, because a late checkout belongs to an earlier day but happened after the review. The Rumo raio-x still reads the day priority lists by day, so the day a Revisão de Rumo closes counts in both periods. Availability is computed on read by `loadReviewAvailability` with the pure functions of `domain/reviews.ts`: checkouts are distinct days with `day.checkoutCompleted` after the last Revisão de Foco; Revisões de Foco are closes after the last Revisão de Rumo; the signals and their numbers come from spec/rituais.md. `Settings.weeklyReflection` gives way to `reviewCadence` (partial, merged with `DEFAULT_REVIEW_CADENCE`); old records keep the field unread, so no migration is needed. The notice joins the queue of `pickTopNotice` as `reviewAvailable`, last; its "Lembrar depois" is kept in `localStorage` like the other snoozes, as the moment it was pressed, and the notice comes back when a checkout happens after it. Old links `#/reflexao/…` and `#/mes/…` open `#/foco` and `#/rumo`. Stored names that mention the reflection (`awaitingReflection`, the notes destination `weeklyReflection`, `task.deferredToReflection`, `section.notesDeferredToReflection`, `reflection.closed`) stay as they are; only the interface changed names.
**Why:** The spec removed the calendar from the reviews (spec/rituais.md, "Duas revisões, sem calendário"): only the checkout has a fixed time. A fixed id for the open review keeps the write path inside a transaction simple. Computing availability on read, from events and records, needs no new stored state and cannot drift. Renaming stored fields would need a migration of data and backups for no gain to the user.
**Updates:** ADR-019 (reflections keyed by week) and ADR-021 (weekly metrics by the event's day).
**Superseded by ADR-060** (2026-10-17): both reviews left the app. Closed reviews stay in `reflections` as history, with no screen.
