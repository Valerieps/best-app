# ADR-063: An own undo history for each line

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Decision:** Each `BlockLine` keeps its own undo history of the line's text and selection (`src/ui/dayNote/lineHistory.ts`, pure and tested). Ctrl/Cmd + Z undoes and Ctrl/Cmd + Shift + Z or Ctrl/Cmd + Y redoes; the keys are always intercepted on the line, and the browser's `historyUndo` / `historyRedo` input events (Edit menu) are routed to the same functions. Typing is grouped into one step until a pause of one second; formats, pastes, mention choices and leaving a list are a step each. A split (Enter), merge or delete (Backspace), kind change (`[] `) and a multi-line paste reset the line's history. We did not keep the native history and did not build a note-wide stack.
**Why:** The text is controlled React state and the formats replace the whole value, which breaks the textarea's native history, and its undo could act on the wrong thing and move the focus. Keeping native history would need `execCommand('insertText')`/`setRangeText` for every format, which is deprecated and behaves differently across browsers, and still would not cover pastes handled by us. A note-wide stack would also undo structural operations (split, merge, delete, paste), but those are several records written through the store, with kind changes, events and optimistic drafts: undoing them correctly needs inverse operations for each. That is left in the roadmap; the per-line history fixes the reported cases (format, typing, paste into the line) at a small cost.
