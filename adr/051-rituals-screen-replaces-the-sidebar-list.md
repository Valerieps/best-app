# ADR-051: The "Rituais" screen replaces the rituals list in the sidebar

**Date:** 2026-10-15
**Decision:** "Rituais" is a sidebar place with its own route, `{ name: 'rituals', assistant? }`, at `#/rituais`, with each assistant at `#/rituais/<slug>`. This route replaces `{ name: 'cleanup' }`, and the old `#/ordem` links still open it. The screen (`ui/cleanup/RitualsScreen.tsx`, renamed from `CleanupScreen.tsx`) shows a month calendar (`ui/cleanup/RitualsCalendar.tsx`), then the "Assistentes" grid. The pure function `domain/ritualsCalendar.ts` gives each day its state: pending, today, closed, empty or future. Pending days come from `loadPendingDays`, as before. Closed days are the past days with at least one line, from the new `loadDaysWithNotes(reader, month)`, which reads the month day by day with `sectionsOfDay`. The Revisão de Foco card is drawn by the screen, not by `assistantCards`: it is not an assistant with an age, and its color is green or yellow depending on whether the review is available (`focusReviewStatus`). The "Dias em aberto" assistant keeps its id, limits and route, but the grid hides its card. `sidebarPlace` maps the checkout, the reviews and the assistants to `rituals`, so "Rituais" stays marked on every screen opened from it.
**Why:** The owner replaced the expandable list with a screen (spec/assistentes.md, "A tela Rituais"). Keeping the assistant ids and settings avoids a migration of `cleanupAgeLimits`. Reading the month day by day keeps the `Reader` port unchanged, and a month is at most 31 indexed lookups, which is fast enough in IndexedDB.
