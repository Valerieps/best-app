# ADR-045: Cleanup colors measure the age of the oldest item

**Date:** 2026-10-14
**Decision:** The color of each card in "Colocar ordem na casa" comes from how many days the assistant's oldest item has been waiting, no longer from how many items it found (`src/domain/cleanupStatus.ts`). Each assistant reports its count and the day its oldest item started waiting: the oldest pending day for "Dias em aberto", the day of the oldest unlinked section for "Seções soltas", and, for "Projetos repetidos", the creation of the second project of a group, when the repeat appeared. A card is green with nothing to clean or while that age is under the yellow limit, so closing yesterday this morning is not late. The limits are still three numbers per assistant in the settings, now in days, saved in a new field, `cleanupAgeLimits`; the old `cleanupLimits`, counted in items, stays in saved records and backups but nothing reads it. The defaults are 2, 4 and 7 days for "Dias em aberto", and 7, 30 and 60 days for the other two.
**Why:** The spec changed the meaning of the color (spec/assistentes.md, "Como funcionam"): the cost of cleaning grows with age, because the context fades, and "Colocar ordem na casa" has no date, so the color is what tells when to go there. A new field avoids reading item counts as days, which would color every card wrong for anyone who had changed the limits, and needs no migration of the settings or of the backup format. The defaults are guesses, like the deadlines in the spec's "Em aberto". This replaces the count-based part of ADR-028; computing the status, rather than storing it, still holds.
